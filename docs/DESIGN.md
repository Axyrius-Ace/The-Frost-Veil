# FROST VEIL: Design Notes (SPOILERS)

## Premise
Hollowpine, Dec 21st. Snowed in for eleven days. Mara Linden, the postmistress, is found frozen against the town clock at 00:45 by Jonah the plow driver. Dr. Brandt called it "exposure."

## The truth
Last winter Dr. Ilse Brandt gave three fever patients fatal doses of Morphenol ("mercy," she calls it). Mara steamed open letters and found out. She hid a torn register page behind the church hymn board and wrote to the valley magistrate. At 21:00 she went to the clinic to confront Brandt. Brandt sedated her, waited for the 23:30 plow pass, dragged her to the square in the storm (Henrik saw a small figure with a lantern), and propped her against the clock. Mara's watch froze at 11:52. Brandt walked back in her star-tread boots, size 37, and hid them behind the curtain.

## Evidence (★ = hidden, flashlight only)
Town square: Wristwatch (11:52), ★Needle puncture, Grey scarf "O.L." · Street: ★Star-tread prints → clinic · Post office: ★Torn letter · Clinic: Morphenol register, ★Wet boots · Inn: Guest book, Debt notice · Church: ★Hidden register page · Jonah: Plow log (ask about his route, then for the log).

## Deductions
- **Means:** needle + register → sedated with clinic Morphenol
- **Motive:** torn letter + hidden page → the fever deaths were overdoses by "I.B."
- **Opportunity:** prints + boots → the trail ends at the clinic
- **Timeline:** plow log + watch → placed between 23:30 and 23:52
- **Alibi:** guest book + watch → Henrik cleared
- **Red herring:** scarf + debt → "Oskar did it." Contradicted by Oskar/Henrik's scarf story or the forgiven debt

## Endings
| Accusation | Condition | Ending |
|---|---|---|
| Brandt | means + motive + opportunity | **The Thaw** (true) |
| Brandt | anything less | Thin Ice |
| Oskar / Henrik / Jonah | — | Cold Blood / Bitter Draught / Snowblind |
| Leave on the bus | after meeting the sheriff | Whiteout |

The accusation unlocks at 2+ deductions, so players can commit early and get it wrong.

## Pacing targets
First clue < 2 min. Full solve 25–40 min. Battery tension: the 9 batteries give ~15 min of beam in total. Enough if you're careful, stressful if you leave it on.
